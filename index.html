Metric guide
CAGR
Average yearly growth rate, compounding included. A 15% CAGR over 10 years turns $10,000 into about $40,000.
Max drawdown
Worst fall from a peak before recovering. −30% means you were down 30% from the top at the scariest moment.
Excess
How much this beat or lagged the S&P 500, in percentage points.
Sharpe
Return per unit of risk. Above 1 is good, above 2 is excellent.
